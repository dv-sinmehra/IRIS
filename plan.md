## Required source-of-truth boundary
The current site is a static client-side prototype. Its only scenario records are the hand-authored constants in `src/data/scenario.ts`; derived values are local TypeScript arithmetic. No Google Earth Engine feed, live meteorological feed, Gemini service, external geospatial registry, municipal notification, SMS/email/radio channel, or insurance product is connected. All records, scores, asset exposures, forecast-like values, dates, thresholds and action prompts are illustrative placeholders. They are unvalidated, not calibrated, not a forecast, and not suitable for operational decisions. The interface must make that provenance and limitation prominent and must not imply that generated photos are satellite observations or event evidence.
